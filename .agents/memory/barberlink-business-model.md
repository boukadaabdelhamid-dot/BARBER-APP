---
name: BarberLink business model and support
description: The intended booking-fee rollout, payment readiness, and support channel.
---

BarberLink is intended to be free at first, then earn revenue through a percentage on each booking. The customer chose to prepare for online payment later, not to collect money now. Algeria is now included among the supported customer countries, alongside France, UAE (Dubai), Qatar, and Saudi Arabia. Customer support should use WhatsApp. The platform payout country is undecided.

**Why:** These are explicit product and rollout decisions from the user.

**How to apply:** Keep current bookings free of online charges. Country switches are saved preferences only; do not collect payments until the platform payout country and provider are established. Keep customer support on WhatsApp; do not invent or reuse a barber's personal number as the support contact.
