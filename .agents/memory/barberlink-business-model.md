---
name: BarberLink business model and support
description: The intended booking-fee rollout, payment readiness, and support channel.
---

BarberLink is intended to be free at first, then earn revenue through a percentage on each booking. The customer chose to prepare for online payment later, not to collect money now. Customer support should use WhatsApp.

**Why:** These are explicit product and rollout decisions from the user.

**How to apply:** Keep current bookings free of online charges. Do not enable payment collection without a separate user decision and chosen provider. Keep customer support on WhatsApp; do not invent or reuse a barber's personal number as the support contact.
